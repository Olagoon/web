Drop-stitch core construction uses thousands of polyester threads connecting the top and bottom PVC layers. When inflated to 10-15 PSI, these threads create a rigid, flat structure that performs like a rigid pool wall.

This technology enables OLagoon pools to be packed into a compact carry bag while providing the rigidity needed for stable swimming, sitting on the edge, and years of backyard use.
