OLagoon has completed a series of supply chain investments that secure raw materials, expand production capacity, and shorten delivery times for customers in more than 50 countries.

### Vertically Integrated Manufacturing

At the heart of the supply chain is OLagoon's 12,000 m² manufacturing campus in Qingdao, China, where more than 200 workers run cutting, lamination, welding, and assembly under one roof. Vertical integration means every drop-stitch pool — from the 2 m Compact Plunge to the 5 m Grand Lagoon — is built end-to-end in a facility holding ISO 9001, CE, and BSCI certifications, with an annual capacity of 15,000 units.

### Secured Material Partnerships

- **High-tensile PVC** sourced through long-term agreements with German and Korean materials suppliers, locking in quality and price stability
- **Drop-stitch fabric** produced on dedicated looms reserved for OLagoon's 16,000-threads-per-square-meter specification
- **Filtration and pump components** qualified through a two-stage supplier audit program

### Faster Global Logistics

New consolidated freight agreements have cut average door-to-door delivery times by 20% for North American and European orders, while regional warehousing now stocking fast-moving SKUs enables spare parts dispatch within 48 hours.

### Looking Ahead

"Our 2025 supply chain program is about dependability," said the OLagoon operations director. "When a family orders a pool in spring, or an OEM partner commits to a fall production run, we need to deliver on time, every time. These investments make that possible."
