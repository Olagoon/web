### Miami Bound

OLagoon will exhibit at the 2026 Miami International Boat Show, February 11–15 at the Miami Marine Stadium Park and Basin. This marks OLagoon's debut at North America's largest boat show, bringing yacht-grade drop-stitch engineering to the backyard leisure market.

The OLagoon booth (B-312) will feature the full product lineup with live demonstrations:

- **Compact Plunge** — 2 m × 1.5 m plunge pool for two
- **Family Oasis** — 3 m × 2 m family pool with integrated seating
- **Grand Lagoon** — 5 m × 2.5 m flagship swim lounge
- **Sand Filter Pump** — 1,500 gal/h cartridge filtration system
- **Thermal Cover & Entry Steps** — heat-retention and safe-access accessories

Live fill-and-float demos will run daily at the booth, letting attendees watch a Grand Lagoon go from folded box to swimmable in under 30 minutes. OLagoon executives will also be available for B2B meetings with potential dealers, resort operators, and OEM partners.

"We are thrilled to bring OLagoon to the Miami Boat Show audience," said the OLagoon sales director. "The show attracts serious marine professionals who immediately recognize the quality of yacht-grade drop-stitch construction when they see it."

Visit olagoon.com/miami-boat-show to schedule a meeting or reserve a demo slot.
