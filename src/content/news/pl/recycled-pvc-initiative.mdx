### Closing the Loop

OLagoon has announced a major sustainability initiative: a closed-loop PVC recycling program that repurposes production waste into new drop-stitch pools.

The program captures PVC trimmings, rejected blanks, and end-of-life test units from the manufacturing floor. These materials are ground, reprocessed, and blended with virgin PVC at up to 30% recycled content without compromising the drop-stitch core integrity.

"Our R&D team spent 18 months developing a reprocessing method that maintains the tensile strength and airtightness standards our customers expect," said the head of manufacturing. "The result is a pool that performs identically but carries a significantly lower environmental footprint."

Early results indicate a 40% reduction in PVC waste sent to landfill, with a target of 60% by 2028. The recycled-content pools are being phased into the Family Oasis product line beginning Q3 2026.

The initiative aligns with OLagoon's ISO 14001 environmental management certification and supports the company's broader commitment to sustainable manufacturing.
