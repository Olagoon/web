OLagoon is a pioneer in drop-stitch inflatable engineering, dedicated to providing families worldwide with safer, more durable, and more enjoyable backyard pools. Our state-of-the-art Research and Development (R&D) center is at the heart of this mission, where the future of inflatable pool technology is forged.

### Mission of Our R&D Center

- **Technological Innovation** — Continuously explore new materials, structures, and manufacturing processes to enhance pool performance and durability
- **User Experience** — Deeply understand how families actually use their pools and design products that are easier to set up, enter, and enjoy
- **Environmental Design** — Employ eco-friendly materials and manufacturing processes to minimize our environmental impact
- **Safety Assurance** — Ensure product safety through rigorous testing and certification

### Key Focus Areas

**Material Innovation:** Explore novel PVC formulations to improve abrasion resistance, UV resistance, and low-temperature performance. Integrate advanced composites and eco-friendly substances to ensure pools are both high-performing and sustainable through years of sun, chlorine, and season changes.

**Structural Design:** Optimize drop-stitch density and air chamber layout to enhance wall rigidity and load-bearing capacity. Perfect the 20 cm high walls, reinforced rails, and stable bases that let a Grand Lagoon hold five adults without flex. Utilize sophisticated CAD software and robotic manufacturing to craft precise shapes and dynamic structures.

**Manufacturing Processes:** Improve the strength and airtightness of heat-seamed and welded seams. Introduce automated cutting and lamination lines to enhance production efficiency and consistency across our 12,000 m² facility.

### Commitment to Sustainability

Environmental stewardship is a core value at OLagoon. Our R&D efforts are geared towards minimizing our ecological footprint through sustainable manufacturing practices and the use of recyclable materials. We are committed to creating products that protect and preserve the natural environments our customers love.

### Global Perspective

We monitor global trends in the outdoor living and pool markets and promptly incorporate the latest technologies and design concepts into our products. Our team of experienced engineers, designers, and pool enthusiasts brings together diverse expertise to set industry standards for quality, performance, and sustainability.
