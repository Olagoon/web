### Miami Bound

olagoon will exhibit at the 2026 Miami International Boat Show, February 11-15 at the Miami Marine Stadium Park and Basin. This marks olagoon's debut at North America's largest boat show.

The olagoon booth (B-312) will feature the full product lineup with hands-on demonstrations:

- **SUP Explorer 11** — All-around beginner inflatable SUP
- **SUP Tour 12** — Touring inflatable SUP with displacement hull
- **SUP Pro 14** — Performance touring SUP for advanced paddlers
- **SUP Yoga 10** — Wide stable SUP for yoga and fitness
- **Life Vest Pro** — SOLAS-certified automatic life vest

On-water demos will be available by appointment, allowing attendees to test olagoon SUPs on the Venetian Basin. olagoon executives will also be available for B2B meetings with potential dealers and fleet operators.

"We are thrilled to bring olagoon to the Miami Boat Show audience," said the olagoon sales director. "The show attracts serious boaters and marine professionals who appreciate quality construction and thoughtful design."

Visit olagoon.com/miami-boat-show to schedule a meeting or book an on-water demo.
