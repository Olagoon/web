### Stronger. Lighter. Stiffer.

OLagoon R&D has developed Drop-Stitch 2.0, a next-generation core material that increases pool wall rigidity by 25% while reducing overall weight by 10% compared to standard drop-stitch construction.

The innovation comes from a denser thread count — 16,000 threads per square meter versus the industry standard of 12,000 — combined with a new high-tensile PVC formulation developed in partnership with a German materials supplier.

"Drop-stitch construction has been the gold standard for inflatable structures for a decade," said the OLagoon R&D director. "Drop-Stitch 2.0 is a genuine advancement that keeps our pools rock-solid at full inflation while maintaining all the portability and easy-storage advantages of inflatable designs."

The new core material will debut in the Grand Lagoon flagship pool and a forthcoming premium rectangular series. Pools built with Drop-Stitch 2.0 are rated to 18 PSI, comfortably above the 10–15 PSI everyday operating range, and exhibit less wall flex even when fully occupied.

Production tooling for Drop-Stitch 2.0 has been installed at the OLagoon manufacturing facility in Qingdao, with first production units expected to ship in Q4 2026.
