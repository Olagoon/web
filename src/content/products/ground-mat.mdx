Your pool base is only as reliable as what is underneath it. One sharp stone, root, or deck screw can end the season — and most inflatables ship with no real base protection.

The OLagoon Protective Ground Mat is a multi-layer woven cloth rated far above standard tarps for puncture resistance, cut larger than the pool footprint so the walls never touch bare ground. It also insulates the pool floor from cold ground and gives you a clean folding surface at pack-down time.
