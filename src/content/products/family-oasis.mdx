The Family Oasis is our best-selling pool for a reason: it delivers genuine family swimming — 3 m × 2 m of it — without construction, permits, or a five-figure budget.

The high-density drop-stitch core inflates to 10–15 PSI, turning the 20 cm walls into stiff benches the adults can sit on while the kids play. The integrated plumbing port accepts the OLagoon sand-filter pump for clear, circulating water all summer, and the UV-resistant PVC fabric is rated for full-season exposure. Unroll it on any firm, level surface, inflate in about 15 minutes, and fill with a garden hose.
