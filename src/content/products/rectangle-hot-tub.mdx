The Rectangle Hot Tub is a real spa, not a wading pool with a heater. A built-in 5 mm high-density insulation layer under the floor blocks ground chill, speeds up heating, cuts energy costs, and cushions every step — while the high-pressure drop-stitch walls stay rock-solid even at 40°C (104°F).

Integrated rigid drop-stitch seats stay firmly in place without floating, giving you ergonomic support and a true luxury soaking posture. The space-saving rectangular design fits seamlessly into patio corners and decks, and thin rigid walls maximize interior legroom and capacity. Dual ports are ready for external heaters, filters, or chillers, so the same tub handles winter soaks and summer cold plunges.
