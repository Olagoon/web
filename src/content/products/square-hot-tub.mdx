The Square Hot Tub takes the same four-season spa engineering as the Rectangle Hot Tub and reshapes it for tight spaces. The corner-friendly square footprint tucks into deck corners and small patios, while thin rigid drop-stitch walls maximize interior capacity.

The built-in 5 mm thermal insulated bottom blocks ground chill, speeds up heating, reduces energy consumption, and cushions every step. Integrated rigid seats stay in place without floating, and the walls hold rock-solid even at 40°C (104°F). Dual ports accept external heaters, filters, or chillers — winter spa nights and summer cold plunges, one tub.
