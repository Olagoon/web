The Compact Plunge brings yacht-grade drop-stitch engineering to the smallest outdoor spaces. Inflated to 10–15 PSI, its 20 cm walls lock ruler-straight — rigid enough to sit on the edge — while the 2 m × 1.5 m footprint fits patios, decks, and modest gardens that a rigid plunge tub could never call home.

Use it for daily cold-plunge recovery, a two-person soak on a summer evening, or as a seasonal spa you fold into a storage bag at summer's end. No plumbing, no tools, no permits: unroll, inflate with the electric pump, connect the sand-filter system, and fill.
