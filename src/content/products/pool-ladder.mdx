Most pool ladders pinch, rub, or wobble against thick inflatable walls. The OLagoon Pool Ladder is engineered for drop-stitch pools specifically: a wide 633 mm inner span and 1022 mm base easily clear 10–15 cm inflatable walls without pinching or rubbing.

Outward-curved bottom legs with thick protective rubber caps keep the ladder stable while protecting the PVC pool floor from scratches or punctures. High U-shaped handrails and crossbars — 1620 mm total height with ø25 mm tubing and reinforcing bars — provide secure, wobble-free support when entering or exiting, and four wide textured steps per side grip wet bare feet. The corrosion-resistant build shrugs off outdoor and chlorinated water, and KD flat packing makes container loading easy alongside pool shipments.
