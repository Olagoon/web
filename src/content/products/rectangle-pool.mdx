The Rectangle Plunge pairs high-strength drop-stitch walls with the plumbing other inflatable pools lack. Dual water inlet/outlet ports connect straight to a sand filter for daily swimming or a water chiller for cold-plunge therapy — one pool, both worlds.

The walls stay firm for sitting or leaning without deformation, and rapid inflation means full setup in just a few minutes. When you are done for the season, the whole pool packs down compactly for effortless transport and storage.
