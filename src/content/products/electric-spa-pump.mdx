An OLagoon hot pool tub is only as good as the water moving through it. The OLagoon Electric SPA Pump is the matched powerplant: one unit that heats, filters, and circulates, with dedicated connectors for each job and an inflatable wave pipe for a gentle massage current.

The standard LQ177 runs the show from the pump panel; the LQ177WIFI adds app control, so the water is hot before you unzip the door. Pool connecting pipes and a drain hose ship in the box, matching the dual ports on every OLagoon hot pool tub.
