The Square Plunge squeezes the most swim area out of the least yard. A square footprint wastes no corner, so patios, side returns, and small gardens get a full-size drop-stitch pool instead of an oversized bathtub.

Like the Rectangle Plunge, it is filter and chiller ready: dual water inlet/outlet ports accept a sand filter for everyday clarity or a chiller for cold-plunge therapy. The high-strength drop-stitch walls stay rigid for sitting or leaning, setup takes minutes, and the deflated pool folds down compactly for the off-season.
