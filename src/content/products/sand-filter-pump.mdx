Standing pool water turns green within days. The OLagoon Sand Filter Pump System keeps water clear all season: it circulates the pool through a silica-sand bed that traps particles down to 20–40 microns and returns clean water via the matched fittings.

The 6-way valve switches between filter, backwash, rinse, and drain modes — maintenance is a knob turn, not a cartridge purchase. One bag of sand replaces a summer of replacement cartridges and lasts about five seasons.
