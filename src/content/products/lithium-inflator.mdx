Inflating a drop-stitch pool to full rigidity takes real pressure, and the nearest outlet is not always where the pool is. The OLagoon Lithium-Battery Electric Inflator cuts the cord: an 11.1V 6000mAh rechargeable lithium pack drives two flow stages — 350 L/min to fill the walls fast, then 70 L/min in high-pressure mode to top them off anywhere in the 0–16 PSI range with auto shut-off at your target.

Set the pressure, press start, and walk away — the inflator stops itself, so walls reach spec without over-pressure. Available in HT-790, HT-790D, and HT-790Plus models for every OLagoon pool and hot pool tub.
