An open pool loses most of its solar-heated warmth overnight and collects leaves, pollen, and insects while you sleep. The OLagoon Thermal Pool Cover fixes both: a bubble-insulation layer floats on the water surface, cutting evaporative and convective heat loss so morning water stays noticeably warmer.

The UV-stabilized top layer sheds debris, and perimeter weights keep the cover seated in the wind. Each cover is custom-cut to the exact footprint of your OLagoon model.
