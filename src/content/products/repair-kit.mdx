Small punctures and seam nicks are part of pool life; draining the season over them is not. The OLagoon PVC Repair Kit ships with every pool and covers the common cases: PVC patches for pinholes and cuts, compatible adhesive for durable bonds, and valve accessories for the fittings.

Find the leak with soapy water, clean the area with alcohol, patch, press, and wait — the repair is permanent and the pool goes straight back into service. See our Inflatable Care & Repair guide for the full walkthrough.
