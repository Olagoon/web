The Round Hot Tub is the social centerpiece of the OLagoon spa line. Reinforced 15 cm drop-stitch walls distribute pressure evenly through a full 360°, delivering rock-solid edge support, a wide armrest rim, and superior side-wall heat retention — while the built-in 5 mm thermal insulated bottom blocks ground chill, speeds up heating, and cushions every step.

Integrated non-floating curved seats wrap the perimeter in a 360° face-to-face layout, so nobody sits with their back to the conversation. The corner-free round interior drives smooth vortex water circulation — even heating and filtration with zero dead zones — and the tub is equally ready for hot spa heating and cold plunges.
