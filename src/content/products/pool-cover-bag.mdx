A pool that sits uncovered collects leaves, pollen, and insects overnight — and a pool stored loose in a shed collects scratches and punctures all winter. The OLagoon Pool Cover & Storage Bag set solves both halves of pool ownership.

The fitted cover goes on between swims to keep debris out of the water, and the heavy-duty storage bag swallows the deflated, folded pool for the off-season, protecting the PVC shell in transit and storage. Both are matched to each OLagoon pool model.
