The Round Lagoon is the classic backyard circle, built like no ring pool you have owned. Its high-density drop-stitch core and reinforced PVC shell inflate to 4–6 PSI, so the walls stand ruler-straight and stable instead of bulging and wobbling — solid enough to lean on, safe enough for the whole family.

Operating pressure stays between 4 and 6 PSI (0.28–0.41 bar) with an absolute maximum of 6 PSI, and the pool handles water from 0 to 40°C — cold-fill summer swims or heated soaks with an external heater. When the season ends, deflate, fold, and store it in a fraction of its filled footprint.
