Climbing over a 64 cm pool wall is hard for children and grandparents, and wet PVC walls are slippery. The OLagoon Inflatable Entry Steps give everyone a stable way in and out.

Built with the same 10–15 PSI drop-stitch construction as the pools, the steps form a rigid two-tier staircase with handrail loops for a firm grip. Snap-on connectors lock the base to the pool wall, and the flat top deck doubles as a poolside bench for two adults.
